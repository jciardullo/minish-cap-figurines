# How to spend your Mysterious Shells

**Best practical choice if you can keep one small table handy:** use the compact strategy below. Its worst-tested expected-time penalty was **8.14% PAL / 7.56% NTSC-U** relative to the corresponding mathematical optimum.

**If you want a rule to memorize without a table:** use the shorter knee strategy. The “knee” is the automatically selected point on the tested simplicity–performance frontier where the predeclared complexity metric judged additional rule complexity to have diminishing returns. It is the memorized option, not the default practical recommendation. It is simpler, but its worst-tested penalty was **22.78% PAL / 21.10% NTSC-U**. The knee is the result of the predeclared complexity metric; it is not automatically the best practical choice for every player.

Neither requires future chest contents, hidden RNG information or an exact walkthrough. Collect completionist rewards normally, without deliberately avoiding shell chests. Use ordinary town visits rather than special early gallery detours.

All percentages mean the displayed chance **before increasing the wager, at the default 1-shell wager**. “100%” means increase the wager until the machine displays 100%; use its preview, without an external calculator.

**“All figurines eligible”** means you have beaten the game and finished the optional progression, exploration and Kinstone fusions/rewards that unlock the remaining figurines. If you are still doing major completionist side content, treat yourself as pre-full-pool. Exclude rewards that themselves require finishing the gallery. The ending alone is insufficient, and the machine does not provide a separate full-pool indicator; this is a conservative completionist milestone, not a chest-order lookup.


**Spoiler-light full-eligibility checklist:**

- Ending completed.
- Completionist Kinstone fusions/rewards capable of unlocking figurines completed.
- Remaining optional progression and side content capable of unlocking figurines completed.
- Gallery-dependent rewards excluded from this check.

There is no direct full-pool indicator; this is not an exhaustive walkthrough.

## Recommended one-page strategy: compact table

Before all figurines are eligible, start a session at an ordinary town visit only when the regional conditions below hold **and your held shells are strictly greater than the reserve for your current phase**:

- **PAL / European:** at least **700 shells**, and default one-shell displayed chance **at least 20%**.
- **NTSC-U:** at least **800 shells**, and default one-shell displayed chance **at least 20%**.

Check all three conditions only when starting the session: the regional shell threshold, the regional chance threshold, and shells strictly above the current row’s reserve. Once started, continue using the current row even if the chance falls below 20%.


**Entry flow (read at an ordinary visit):**

1. All figurines eligible? **Yes:** use the final row. **No:** continue below.
2. Regional shell threshold met (700 PAL / 800 NTSC-U)? If no, continue progression.
3. Default one-shell chance at least 20%? If no, continue progression.
4. Shells strictly above this phase’s reserve? If no, continue progression.
5. If all three early-entry tests pass, start and follow the current phase row. After each completed pull, stop before another pull once shells are at or below reserve.

**Water → Wind note:** the 800-shell reserve is the binding shell condition. Being strictly above it automatically satisfies PAL’s separate 700-shell entry threshold. Keep all three general entry checks.

**Table legend:** `>x%` means the default one-shell displayed chance is strictly greater than x%; otherwise raise the display to the listed 100% branch.

**Reserve = the shell amount at which you stop the early session.** If held shells are already at or below it, do not start another pull. A completed pull may take you below the reserve; the reserve does not reduce that pull's prescribed wager.

| Recognizable phase | PAL reserve / wager | NTSC-U reserve / wager |
|---|---|---|
| Before completing Fortress of Winds | 600 / 1 shell if >80%; otherwise 100% | 600 / 1 shell if >80%; otherwise 100% |
| Fortress complete, before Water Element | 150 / 100% | 100 / 100% |
| Water Element, before Wind Element | 800 / 100% | 800 / 100% |
| Wind Element, before all eligibility | 0 / 1 shell if >15%; otherwise 100% | 0 / 1 shell if >20%; otherwise 100% |
| All figurines eligible | 0 / 1 shell if >8%; otherwise 100% | 0 / 1 shell if >9%; otherwise 100% |

**If the prescribed wager is unaffordable, wager all held shells.** Never farm or buy shells during an early session. After all eligibility, restock when empty: buy as many bundles as your current rupees already afford, up to **three PAL / four NTSC-U**. If none are affordable, farm only the missing money for the full batch: **900 R / 800 R**, then buy **90 / 120 shells**. Purchased bundles must fit below the 999-shell cap.

**Threshold boundaries:** “greater than” is strict. At exactly 80%, 20%, 15%, 10%, 9%, 8%, 7% or 50%, the corresponding one-shell/100% rule chooses 100%. Entry tests are inclusive: exactly 20% (compact), 50% (PAL knee), or 60% (NTSC-U knee) qualifies when the other entry conditions hold. The NTSC-U early knee always raises to 100% once its session starts.

This is a complete strategy. Do not combine its entry conditions or wagers with the memorized rules below.

## Memorize-it strategy: empirical knee

### PAL / European

During normal progression, use ordinary town visits. Start a session only when the default one-shell displayed chance is **at least 50%**. Once started, continue even after it falls below 50%: **wager 1 shell if the default one-shell displayed chance is greater than 50%; otherwise raise the display to 100%**. If you cannot afford 100%, wager all held shells. Stop when empty; do not farm or buy during these early sessions.

Once every figurine is eligible, **wager 1 shell if the default one-shell displayed chance is greater than 7%; otherwise raise the display to 100%**. If short of that wager, use all held shells. When empty, aim for a **full 90-shell restock**: if you have less than **900 R**, farm until you reach 900 R, then buy **three bundles**. This rule tops up to the full batch even if you could already afford one or two bundles. If already funded for all three, go directly to the shop.

### NTSC-U

During normal progression, use ordinary town visits. Start a session only when the default one-shell displayed chance is **at least 60%**. Once started, continue even after it falls below 60%, always raising the display to **100%**. Stop when that wager is unaffordable; do not farm or buy during these early sessions.

Once every figurine is eligible, **wager 1 shell if the default one-shell displayed chance is greater than 10%; otherwise raise the display to 100%**. Restock before the next pull if its wager is unaffordable. If you can already buy at least one bundle, buy as many as your current rupees afford, up to **four bundles**. Otherwise farm only the missing money needed for **800 R**, then buy **four bundles (120 shells)**.

## When shells approach 999

Use an ordinary Carlov visit if your chosen strategy's entry conditions are satisfied. Otherwise continue normal progression. **These rules deliberately tolerate some overflow; they do not promise to save every shell.** A low chance can make an expensive early session worse than accepting overflow.

## What the simplification costs

**Do-nothing-clever baseline:** wait until all figurines are eligible, then always wager 1 shell. At restocking, buy the maximum permitted batch: **three PAL bundles (90 shells, 900 R)** or **four NTSC-U bundles (120 shells, 800 R)**. Farm the missing money even if one or more bundles are already funded; if the whole batch is funded, go directly to the shop. Respect wallet and shell caps.

Supplemental audit ID 10000 takes **173.67 modeled minutes PAL / 173.67 NTSC-U** on the reference route. The recommended compact saves **51.63 / 58.38 minutes**, respectively. Its worst-tested regret is **64.21% PAL / 71.64% NTSC-U**; the reference time does not establish robustness across routes. This is an observable model baseline, distinct from the future-informed one-shell benchmark. Consultation time is unmeasured.

**Supplemental check:** a local compact-policy mutation neighborhood found an NTSC-U variant at **7.47%** worst-tested regret, remembering one additional fixed wager (complexity 39 versus 37). Its reference gain is only **8.02 seconds**, so the simpler compact table above remains recommended. No PAL improvement was found. This small neighborhood does not prove the human-policy frontier has flattened.

The **5%** figure remains a predeclared diagnostic against a future-informed optimum, not a fair matched-information threshold. No policy in the original bounded set or supplemental evaluations met it; this says nothing about every conceivable human rule.

On the reference route, the compact table costs **2.63 minutes PAL / 2.51 minutes NTSC-U** versus the mathematical optimum and saves **8.07 minutes PAL / 7.24 minutes NTSC-U** versus the memorized knee. Consultation time is not measured. The regret denominator is a future-informed, scenario-specific optimum; this is not an identified decomposition of information and simplification costs.

Under the supplemental **uniform synthetic eight-route prior**, the recommended compact rule is only **0.152% PAL / 0.052% NTSC-U** slower in mean time than the best restricted same-observation rule found; the knees are **6.72% / 6.20%** slower. The benchmark is a one-wager mutation of compact and is two complexity points more complicated in either region (**42 versus 40 PAL; 39 versus 37 NTSC-U**). This is a local best-tested comparison, not a solved route-blind optimum, so it does not separate information loss from policy-simplification loss. The observed compact excess over this benchmark is a **lower bound** on its excess over the unknown true same-information optimum. These eight-route synthetic-prior means differ from the 14 PAL / 12 NTSC-U robustness-set descriptive means above. Consultation time remains unmeasured. No result here establishes whether a human policy can or cannot meet 5% against a true matched-information optimum.

Times in the middle columns use the reference route. Worst-tested regret covers all regional route and timing cases; it is not the reference percentage. The worst absolute time gaps shrink from **24.09 to 9.87 minutes PAL**, and **21.35 to 8.16 minutes NTSC-U**, when moving from knee to compact.

**Neither option meets the desirable 5% target. No human policy assessed by deterministic forward evaluation met it across its regional test set.** These are the selected rules from the original bounded search family, not globally optimal human strategies; the supplemental US improvement is reported separately above. The compact recommendation is an editorial choice for a player willing to consult a small table; the predeclared empirical-knee criterion and selected IDs remain unchanged, although the supplemental US endpoint changes normalization and was sensitive to modest complexity-weight changes.

The full optimizer and software helper remain maximum-performance benchmarks, separate from these human rules. Memorizing thresholds and consulting a screenshot are different usability modes; the scalar complexity score is not a validated cognitive metric.

## Assumptions

Fast text speed, holding B, 20-second base pulls, 300 rupees/minute house farming, and 15 seconds per purchased bundle. Random drops and ordinary-game rupees are excluded; duplicate refunds are credited and capped. Actual play may need less farming. Menu checks, mistakes, pauses and consultation time are not separately measured. NTSC ±10 input timing is an empirical assumption. The frozen resource reconstruction and route schedules remain conditional; robustness was tested, not proved for every pickup order.

For the mathematical justification, certification limits, regional results and reproducibility files, see [paper.pdf](paper.pdf) and [README.md](README.md). Previous late-cleanup times remain comparisons, not the recommendation for collecting resources as encountered.
